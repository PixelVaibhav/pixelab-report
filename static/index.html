About, privacy and disclaimer

Built to help you ask better questions.

About. Pixelab Report turns medical documents into a visual, plain-language explanation so you can have a more informed conversation with your doctor.

Privacy Policy. Your files are processed in memory, are not saved to disk and are deleted automatically after about 30 minutes. They are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to be read and explained, and Google's own data terms apply to that. DICOM files are converted to pictures; their patient data fields are not sent, but names printed on the image itself may be. Do not upload anything unless you accept this.

Terms. The service is provided as is, without guarantees of accuracy. Always check the highlighted results against your original report.

Medical Disclaimer. Pixelab Report does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It cannot read a scan the way a radiologist does. Results inside a printed range do not prove you are healthy, and results outside it do not prove disease. Please consult a qualified doctor.
